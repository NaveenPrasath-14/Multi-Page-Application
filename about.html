MEET CHEF DOUG

Doug first made the BBQ sauce at work — from scratch — and the reaction was immediate. Guests loved it. Chefs raved. That raised a bigger question: why didn’t anything on the shelf taste like this? He tried them all. None measured up. So he went back to the stove. For the next year, he tested, tweaked, and refined — dialing in the balance of real fruit, heat, and acidity until it locked in. That’s Bucks Sauce — small-batch BBQ sauce built by a chef, not a factory.
